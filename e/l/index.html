What is Events

An event happens once.

Events is a way to document more than the visible moment. I record people, movement, sound, space and sequence so the final result feels closer to the event itself.

Layers

Different sources become one record. Each layer adds information the others cannot capture.

01

PHOTO

The main visual layer. People, details, key moments and atmosphere.

02

VIDEO

Short fragments of movement and real moments. Used where a still image is not enough.

03

AUDIO

Voices, phrases and the sound of the place. Useful moments can become part of the final story.

04

SPACE

The location itself: where people were, how the space looked and how the event moved through it.

05

DATA

Time, sequence and technical information. Small details that help rebuild the event later.

06

TRACE

The final record: selected material arranged as one system instead of separate files.

How it works

I stay inside the event. The system works around it.

01 Before

We discuss the event, important people, timing and what must not be missed.

02 During

I document the event continuously using several visual and audio layers.

03 After

The material is selected, edited and organized into one clear structure.

04 Delivery

You receive finished photographs and the additional layers agreed before the event.
